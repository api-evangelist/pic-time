Pic-Time announced Pic-Time 2.0, a redesign of the client gallery experience covering gallery design, print shop, album creation, and mobile. The release adds over 30 cinematic photo gallery cover designs, including video, animation, and multi-image layouts, aimed at giving photographers a more editorial, immersive presentation for clients.
